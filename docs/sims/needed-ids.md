# Sim ids in use

One line per sim id: `<id> — <leaf id> — <one-line idea> — template: <name>`. The `— template: <name>` suffix is required and names one of the seven ADR 009 §2 templates (`stepper`, `zones`, `lanes`, `flow`, `chart`, `states`, `compare`). The sim itself is the ```` ```sim ```` block in the leaf (ADR 009, decision L37), not a SIM comment marker (SM0 is an error). All 67 rows carry the suffix (21 Oct 2026). A bespoke physics candidate (ADR 009 §5 ★) keeps its template suffix until its bespoke sim replaces it under the same id. Ids are kebab-case, unique, and never change after publishing.
