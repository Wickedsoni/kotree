# Audit 22 Oct 2026: why "most sims do not work", plus other weak spots

VERDICT: FIX

Scope: read-only audit of `feat/design-v2` at a441409. Read ADR 009, the sim runtime (`site/src/jsMain/kotlin/dev/kotree/site/sims/**`, `ui/lesson/SimFrame.kt`, `ui/lesson/LessonView.kt`, `lessons/LessonData.kt`), `buildSrc/.../SimSpec.kt`, CI and deploy files, and the content.

How it was tested:
- Ran `./gradlew :site:kobwebStart` (JDK 21). The build passed and the Kotlin compile printed no `w:` lines.
- Drove headless Chrome 155 over CDP. Each of the 94 sim leaves was opened on `/preview/<id>`. The probe pressed Step through the whole script and recorded the live region, the Try progress and any stage content that falls outside the 720 x 405 box.
- Hand tests: Play, mouse drag, keyboard pick-up, reduced motion and a 390 px viewport.
- Repeated key checks on the live site `https://kotree.garvitmaheshwari.in`.
- Statically scanned all 94 sim specs in the generated lesson JSON against what each layout draws.

What works:
- All 94 sims mount with no console errors or exceptions, locally and live.
- Step, Back and Reset work.
- Mouse drag with pointer capture works (zones, `what-is-a-commit`).
- The reduced-motion fade runs: one 150 ms opacity animation, no transform.
- Try progress lights step by step.
- The data in content is valid: 89 of 94 specs drive only things the layouts draw.

So the engine itself does not crash. A learner sees "nothing happens" for the reasons ranked below.

Legend: Sev = Critical / High / Medium / Low. Size = S (under 20 lines) / M (one file or a few files) / L (a design change or many files). Who = `fixer` (mechanical) or owner (`ui-engineer`, `sim-engineer`, `architect`, `curriculum-designer`, author).

## A. Sim failure root causes (ranked)

1. **Critical. The teaching text of every sim is invisible.**
   - Where: `site/src/jsMain/kotlin/dev/kotree/site/sims/TemplateSimHost.kt:179-183`.
   - Symptom: on most sims, pressing an action or Step seems to do nothing. The sentence that explains each event (`say`), every refusal (`refuse`) and the `intro` live only in a visually hidden `aria-live` region. Sighted users never see them.
   - Measured: 59 of 682 action cases and drops change nothing visible except that hidden text (34 sims). In 3 chart sims every button is say-only: `daa/analysis/big-o-omega-theta`, `dsa/complexity/counting-steps`, `dsa/strings/text-building-cost`.
   - Every refused action is also invisible. Example: "Take snapshot" with an empty tray.
   - Many Try steps say "read the answer", and the answer exists only in the hidden text (`dsa/stacks/what-is-a-stack` step 3, `dsa/complexity/big-o-notation` step 1).
   - Writers already work around it: `foundation/programming-thinking/counting-loops` stores text in a var called `said` just to show it as a readout.
   - Cause: ADR 009 §4 defines the live region only for screen readers. No visible caption or narration line exists.
   - Fix: add a visible narration line, either under the stage or as a stage footer. It shows `intro` at first, then `state.said`, styled as a refusal when `state.refused`. Keep the live region. Size S-M. Who: `ui-engineer`. ADR 009 §4 needs an amendment line (`architect`).

2. **Critical (on phones) / High (on desktop). Stage content is clipped.**
   - Where: `site/src/jsMain/kotlin/dev/kotree/site/ui/lesson/SimFrame.kt:79-89`. The stage has a fixed `aspect-ratio 16/9` and `overflow: hidden`.
   - Symptom, desktop at 1280 px: in 18 sims, content lies outside the 405 px box. Of these, 15 have stage elements outside the visible box and 3 need a scroll inside the stage (`how-a-program-repeats`, `sealed-classes`, `how-art-runs-code`). Clipped sims:
     - `what-is-an-activity`, `running-work-on-another-thread`, `how-arraylist-grows`, `matching-brackets`, `what-is-a-stack`
     - `what-happens-when-i-tap-an-icon`, `what-is-an-os`, `trace-code-by-hand`, `what-is-a-function`, `break-and-continue`
     - `try-catch-finally`, `review-a-generated-file`, `who-would-attack-my-app`, `tools/git/branches`, `arrange-act-assert`
   - Desktop effects: the third lane (where results land), the lit stepper line and the newest log rows are out of view. Stepper code wraps into a one-third column and the log columns overlap.
   - At 390 px wide the stage is about 200 px tall. In `what-is-a-stack` only the first lane shows. In zones sims the labels are cut ("Snapshot shelf").
   - Cause: layouts are sized for 720 x 405 but the stage shrinks with the viewport. The lit line is never scrolled into view.
   - Fix (owner picks one):
     - (a) Let the stage height grow with its content (`min-height` instead of `aspect-ratio`) and stack columns under a breakpoint token.
     - (b) Keep 16:9 but add `scrollIntoView({block: "nearest"})` for the lit line and the newest row, and switch stepper and lanes to one column on narrow widths.
   - Size M-L. Who: `ui-engineer`. Needs `architect` sign-off because ADR 009 §2 says "auto-layout inside the 720 x 405 stage".

3. **High. Play stops one event early.**
   - Where: `TemplateSimHost.kt:138-142`. `stepScript()` already increments `scriptIdx`, then `if (scriptIdx + 1 >= spec.script.size) pause()` runs.
   - Symptom, observed:
     - `safe-call-and-elvis`: Play runs 4 of 5 events and stops, with Step still enabled.
     - `what-is-a-commit`: Play stops after event 4 of 5, so the key "nothing staged, no snapshot" refusal never plays.
   - Every sim's Play demo misses its last (often the payoff) event.
   - Fix: change the check to `if (scriptIdx >= spec.script.size) pause()`. Add a `PlaybackTest` case. Size S. Who: `fixer`.

4. **High. Several layouts silently ignore effects the validator accepts.**
   - Where:
     - `ZonesLayout.kt` (ZoneBox 136-175): ignores `lit`, and ignores marks and labels on zones.
     - `LanesLayout.kt` (LaneRow 119-150): ignores `lit`.
     - `StepperLayout.kt` (LineRow 137-159): ignores `label`, `mark`, items and lit groups or board cells.
     - `ChartLayout.kt`: ignores `light`, `mark` and `label`.
     - `FlowLayout.kt` and `StatesLayout.kt`: ignore labels and marks on stations and nodes.
     - `buildSrc/src/main/kotlin/dev/kotree/build/SimSpec.kt:593-600`: accepts all of these through `anyId`.
   - Symptom: buttons that "do nothing". Affected live sims:
     - `treasure-hunt-clues` (dsa/linked-lists/what-is-a-linked-list)
     - `garden-window-view` (kotlin/lambdas/closures)
     - `locker-row-reach` (dsa/arrays/arrays-in-memory: the whole sim's visible effect is `light`, so the stage never changes)
     - `two-keys-one-flat` (label on a zone)
     - `null-lunch-box` (mark on a zone)
   - 5 sims show the same stage text before and after the full script: `best-worst-average-case`, `arrays-in-memory`, `what-happens-when-i-tap-an-icon`, `two-kinds-of-equality`, `what-is-null`.
   - Fix: either draw lit, mark and label for zones, lanes and places (Size M, `ui-engineer`), or add an SM rule that rejects effects the template cannot draw (Size S-M, `ui-engineer` with `architect`). Then fix the 5 specs (`sim-engineer`).

5. **High. `phone` and `timeline` add-ons are never rendered.**
   - Where: no code references them in `site/.../sims/` except `SimModel.kt:99` (`hasPhone`). `SimSpec.kt:427-440` validates them and ADR 009 WP3 says the host draws them.
   - Symptom: no live sim uses them yet, but `docs/sims/needed-ids.md` maps `main-thread-queue`, `blocked-main-thread-timeline`, `launch-path-manifest`, `declarative-vs-steps` and `hotel-corridor-files` to them. The first Android leaf that uses one will show nothing.
   - Fix: build the add-ons, or make SM2 reject them until they exist. Size M. Who: `ui-engineer`.

6. **High. A keyboard pick-up fails after a mouse drag.**
   - Where: `ZonesLayout.kt:235` and `311-345`. `LanesLayout.kt:246` has the same pattern.
   - Symptom, observed on `what-is-a-commit`: after dragging File A into the tray, pressing Enter on File B (twice) never shows the "Drop in ..." buttons.
   - Cause: the shared `dragMoved` flag is set by the drag. The chip that was dragged unmounts before its `click` arrives, so the flag is never cleared. The next click or Enter on any chip is swallowed. The second failed Enter is observed but its cause is unconfirmed.
   - Fix: clear `moved[0]` in `finish` after a commit, or keep the flag per chip. Size S. Who: `fixer`, with `ui-engineer` re-check.

7. **Medium (code reading, not reproduced). Lanes drag can fire the wrong item id.**
   - Where: `LanesLayout.kt:146` and `245`. Slots are keyed by index, and `wireDrag(el, item.id, ...)` is bound once in `ref`.
   - Symptom: when a slot's occupant changes without a remount (a queue shift, or a move between draggable items), dragging the new chip sends the old item's event.
   - Affects lanes sims with drops: `best-worst-average-case`, `find-a-bug-without-guessing`, `what-is-a-bit`, `who-would-attack-my-app`.
   - Fix: wrap `ItemChip` in `key(item.id)`. Size S. Who: `fixer`.

8. **Medium (code reading). A drop onto an occupied slot hides an item.**
   - Where: `SimCore.kt:125-131` (Move does not check occupancy) and `LanesLayout.kt:59` (`associateBy { it.at }` keeps one item per slot).
   - Fix: make Move into an occupied lane slot refuse. Size S. Who: `ui-engineer`.

9. **Medium. Play can show "Pause" while doing nothing.**
   - Where: `TemplateSimHost.kt:127-136`.
   - Symptom: if Play is pressed while the frame is less than 10 % visible, `visible == false` blocks the timer. No new IntersectionObserver callback arrives, so the label stays "Pause" and nothing runs. This was seen in automation. A user who presses Play and then scrolls away and back gets a restart.
   - Fix: when not visible, set `playing = false` on Play, or scroll the frame into view on Play. Size S. Who: `ui-engineer`.

10. **Medium. The text description contradicts the sim.**
    - Where: `sims/core/TextDescription.kt:19`. `initial(spec, spec.script)` hoists every setting in the script to the start.
    - Symptom: in `safe-call-and-elvis` the description says the empty-house ask "brings back 0". Step shows "no reply" at the same point.
    - Fix: generate the description by folding prefixes, as Step does. Size S. Who: `ui-engineer`.

11. **Medium. Try steps tick even when the action was refused.**
    - Where: `SimCore.kt:10` (decision: refused events stay in history) and `TryProgress.kt:28-31`.
    - Symptom: pressing "Take snapshot" with an empty tray still completes "then press Take snapshot".
    - Fix: keep refused events in the history, but have `TryProgressCalc` skip refused events. The fold already knows which ones they are. Size M. Who: `ui-engineer` + `architect` (ADR 009 §3 wording).

12. **Medium. Step and Play rarely reach the required Try steps.**
    - Where: content, `script:` keys.
    - Symptom: after pressing Step through the whole script, 20 sims still have a required (non-optional) Try step open. Most need a slider or toggle the script never sets. Examples:
      - `time-vs-space` (step 1)
      - `counting-steps`, `text-building-cost`, `big-o-omega-theta`, `what-is-a-cpu`, `counting-loops`, `how-a-program-decides`
      - `how-a-program-repeats`, `loop-over-a-list`, `trace-code-by-hand`, `what-is-a-function`, `ranges-and-for-loops`, `exceptions`
      - `try-catch-finally`, `lateinit`, `safe-call-and-elvis`, `find-the-hot-function`, `arrange-act-assert`, `merge-conflicts`, `what-happens-when-i-build`
    - Fix: extend each `script` so it walks the Try steps in order (≤ 20 events). Size M. Who: `sim-engineer`.

13. **Medium. The chart's x marker only works for a var named `x`, and `big-o-notation` sets x outside the plot.**
    - Where: `ChartLayout.kt:145`. In `content/dsa/complexity/big-o-notation.md` the sim has `scale.x [0, 12]` while "Set n to 1000" sets x = 1000, so the marker vanishes and only the legend text changes.
    - Fix: clamp the marker and show "→ 1000" at the edge, or widen the scale in the spec. Size S. Who: `ui-engineer` / `sim-engineer`.

14. **Medium. Spec read errors are swallowed.**
    - Where: `SimModel.kt:206-208` and `LessonView.kt:252-263`.
    - Symptom: any JSON shape surprise turns a sim into the read-only Try list on a live page, with no console message. This makes future "sim does not work" reports undiagnosable. No current sim hit it: all 94 mounted.
    - Fix: `console.error` with the sim id in the `catch`. Size S. Who: `fixer`.

15. **Low. Smaller sim issues.**
    - The new-chip enter animation lasts about 1.5 s (`ZonesLayout.kt:286-295`, spring duration). Steps run every 1.2 s, so chips look grey mid-fade.
    - The drag hint is colour-only (`ZonesLayout.kt:191`). ADR §3 asks for a dashed outline.
    - After Enter on a chip, focus is not moved to the "Drop in ..." buttons, so a keyboard user must Tab past every chip (`ZonesLayout.kt:132`, `253-256`).
    - Identical consecutive `say` text is not re-announced.
    - Hard-coded literals: `font-weight: 700` (`StatesLayout.kt:219`), SVG stroke widths and dashes (`StatesLayout.kt:147`, `ChartLayout.kt:49`). This breaks hard rule 2 and is not caught by `check-hardcoded.sh`.
    - Size S each. Who: `fixer` / `ui-engineer`.

## G. Accessibility summary

The details are in the items above:
- Sighted users get no narration (1).
- Clipped content fails WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow at 320-390 px (2).
- Keyboard pick-up breaks after a drag (6).
- Focus is not moved to the drop buttons, and the drag hint relies on colour alone (15).
- Reduced motion works.
- Every control is a real button with a label.
- The frame has `role=group` and an `aria-label`.

## Claims not verified

- Items 7 and 8 come from reading the code, not from a reproduction. They rest on Compose HTML running a `ref` callback once per element mount.
- Item 6 is confirmed (two Enter presses failed). The full cause of the second failure is not.
- The phone checks (item 2) used Chrome device emulation at 390 x 900, not a real phone.
- The tree checks in D were not run.
